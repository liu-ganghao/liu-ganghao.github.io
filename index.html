👋About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Technology at the East China University of Science and Technology (ECUST), Shanghai.

My work spans LLM algorithms and AI application engineering: efficient fine-tuning (SFT / PEFT / GRPO), MoE architecture design, inference acceleration, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), agent workflows, and full-stack deployment. I enjoy turning research ideas into shipped products, with hands-on experience in model training platforms, inference acceleration, and end-to-end LLM deployment.
